# The Real Music House 🎵

A modern, responsive, and glassmorphic music web application with an interactive audio player, animated sound visualizer, dynamic playlist switcher, and lossless acoustic streaming.

---

## ✨ Features & Enhancements

- **🎧 Interactive Glassmorphic Music Player:**
  - Real-time seekable timeline progress bar with current & duration timestamps.
  - Playlist switcher supporting multiple local master tracks (`Alan Walker - Faded` & `The Real Music House - Melodic Vibes`).
  - Animated soundwave equalizer that dynamically bounces when music is playing.
  - Continuous rotating vinyl disc artwork animation.
  - Smooth volume control slider and instant one-click mute/unmute.
  - Shuffle and repeat playback modes.
- **🎨 Modern Glassmorphic UI:**
  - Frosted glass cards with `backdrop-filter: blur()`.
  - Neon cyan/purple glow accents and clean Google Fonts (`Poppins` & `Outfit`).
  - Dark gradient overlay for enhanced readability and visual depth.
- **📱 Responsive Across All Devices:**
  - Optimized for desktop, laptop, tablet, and mobile screens.
  - Mobile slide-out navigation menu.
- **⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts:**
  - `Space`: Play / Pause toggle
  - `Arrow Right`: Seek forward 5 seconds
  - `Arrow Left`: Seek backward 5 seconds
  - `M`: Toggle Mute / Unmute
- **📂 Multi-Section Content:**
  - Hero section with live statistics and direct play call-to-actions.
  - Featured Tracks & Products catalog with instant card playback and direct download buttons.
  - About section highlighting studio sound engineering and acoustic specifications.
  - Technical audio hardware specification cards.
  - Contact & newsletter subscription footer.

---

## 🚀 How to Run

1. Open the folder `Music-website/`.
2. Double click `index.html` to open it in any modern web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Firefox, Safari).
3. Click the Play button or press `Space` to start streaming music!
